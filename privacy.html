隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-04

中文

本应用（下称“本应用”）仅供其所有者个人使用，用于访问所有者本人的 Google Drive 文件。

  • 数据收集：本应用不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息。
  • 数据访问：本应用仅在所有者本人的设备上、以所有者本人的授权，访问所有者本人的 Google Drive 内容。
  • 数据共享：本应用不向任何第三方共享、出售或转让任何数据。
  • 数据存储：授权凭据仅保存在所有者本人的设备上，不发送至任何外部服务器。
  • 第三方服务：本应用不集成任何第三方分析、广告或追踪服务。
  • 撤回授权：所有者可随时在 Google 账号的“第三方应用与权限”页面撤销本应用的访问权限；撤销后本应用即无法再访问任何数据。
  • 数据留存：本应用不在任何服务器上留存数据，因此不存在数据删除请求流程。

English

This application (the “App”) is for the personal use of its owner only, to access the owner's own Google Drive files.

  • Data collection: The App collects, stores, and transmits no personal information.
  • Data access: The App accesses the owner's own Google Drive content only, on the owner's own device, under the owner's own authorization.
  • Data sharing: The App shares, sells, or transfers no data to any third party.
  • Data storage: Authorization credentials are stored only on the owner's own device and are never sent to any external server.
  • Third-party services: The App integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • Revoking access: The owner may revoke the App's access at any time from the Google Account “Third-party apps & services” page. After revocation the App can access no data.
  • Data retention: The App retains no data on any server, so no data-deletion request process is required.
Contact: the support email shown on the application's Google authorization screen.
