私有 Google Drive 工具 / Private Google Drive Tool

中文

本应用是一个仅供其所有者个人使用的 Google Drive 访问工具。

它运行在所有者本人的电脑上，用于读写所有者本人的 Google Drive 文件，包括读取 Google 文档、表格与幻灯片。

本应用不向公众提供服务，不收集任何用户数据。授权与数据均留在所有者本人的设备上。

English

This application is a Google Drive access tool for the personal use of its owner only.

It runs on the owner's own computer and reads/writes the owner's own Google Drive files, including Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides.

The application does not serve the public and collects no user data. Authorization and data remain on the owner's own device.
